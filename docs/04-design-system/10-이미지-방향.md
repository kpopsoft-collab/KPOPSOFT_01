## 9. IMAGE DIRECTION

Photography should feel documentary and natural.

Recommended imagery:

- workshop scenes
- small team collaboration
- instructor and participant interaction
- software prototype testing
- whiteboard discussions
- hands using digital devices
- physical prototyping scenes

Photography style:

- candid
- warm
- realistic
- editorial
- slightly imperfect compositions

Avoid:

- people pointing at empty screens
- corporate handshake stock photos
- futuristic holograms
- fake AI robot graphics
- overly staged office photographs
