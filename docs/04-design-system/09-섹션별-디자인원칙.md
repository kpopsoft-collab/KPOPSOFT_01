## 8. SECTION DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Every section should have its own visual rhythm.

Example:


### HERO
Large editorial typography
Geometric brand graphics


### TRUST / PARTNERS
Simple logo strip
Minimal monochrome treatment


### INTRODUCTION

Headline:

배우기만 하는 교육이 아니라,
직접 만드는 경험을 설계합니다.

Use large text and small supporting paragraphs.


### PROGRAMS

Asymmetric colorful program grid.


### SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

Large case-study oriented layout.

Categories:

Web
App
AI Automation
Internal Tools
Digital Product


### AI PROTOTYPE LAB

Experimental section.

Show process:

Idea
→ Prototype
→ Test
→ Build


### EXPERT NETWORK

Introduce instructors and experts.

Do not use generic corporate employee cards.

Use:

- portrait
- expertise tags
- short philosophy quote
- specialization


### PROCESS

Diagram-style horizontal journey.


### CUSTOM TRAINING

Large colored CTA panel.

Message:

조직에 필요한 AI 활용 방식을
함께 설계합니다.


### FINAL CTA

Large statement:

기획서가 없어도,
문제부터 이야기할 수 있습니다.

Contact button.
