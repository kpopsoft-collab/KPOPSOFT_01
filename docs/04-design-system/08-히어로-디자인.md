## 7. HERO DESIGN

The Hero should immediately establish KPOPSOFT as both:

- a software company
- an AI education company


Suggested headline direction:

기술을 배우는 것에서
직접 만드는 것까지.

or

Learn.
Build.
Transform.

or

AI를 배우고,
소프트웨어로 만듭니다.


Hero composition:

Left:
Large bold headline

Supporting copy:
2–3 concise lines

CTA:
프로그램 보기
프로젝트 문의


Right or integrated background:

A composition of colorful geometric shapes representing:

- software
- learning
- connection
- experimentation

Do not use laptop mockups as the primary Hero image.

Do not show generic code screenshots.

Hero should feel like a strong editorial poster.
