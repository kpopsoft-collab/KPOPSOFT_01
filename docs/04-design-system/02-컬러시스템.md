## 2. COLOR SYSTEM

Base Background:

Warm Ivory
#F6F1EA

Primary Text:
#292522

Primary Blue:
#315BDB

Bright Red:
#F04B32

Warm Yellow:
#FFC85C

Soft Coral:
#FF9A95

Mint Green:
#63C7B2

Sky Blue:
#72A3E8

Dark Navy:
#243562

Text-on-tint companions (darkened accents for legible colored text on a pale
tint of the same hue — the base Warm Yellow / Mint Green are too light to read
as text; used e.g. for status badges):

Yellow Ink:
#8A5A12

Mint Ink:
#146A5B


Color rules:

Use warm ivory as the dominant background.

Primary colors should appear in large graphic shapes, cards, diagrams, and selective CTA elements.

Do not use gradients as the primary visual device.

Use flat colors with strong contrast.

Each section may introduce its own dominant accent color while maintaining the same global palette.

Maintain approximately:

60% neutral background
25% white or neutral surfaces
15% strong accent colors
